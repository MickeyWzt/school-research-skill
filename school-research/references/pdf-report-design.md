# PDF Report Design

Use this guide when the final deliverable should be an easy-to-read PDF.

## PDF Structure

1. Cover
   - School name, program, applicant context, entry cycle, report date, and confidence level.
2. Decision summary
   - Bottom-line recommendation, 3-5 best-fit reasons, 3-5 main concerns, and next actions.
3. Fit scorecard
   - Academic fit, admissions competitiveness, financial fit, environment fit, and strategic value.
   - Use short labels such as strong, moderate, weak, unknown, or high-risk.
4. Admissions checklist
   - Required materials, deadline, test policy, English requirement, recommendation letters, essays, interviews, portfolio, and application platform.
5. Timeline
   - Date or period, task, owner, and status.
6. Program and academic analysis
   - Curriculum match, prerequisites, opportunities, and concerns.
7. Cost and aid analysis
   - Tuition, fees, living costs, aid eligibility, scholarship risks, and deposit notes.
8. Student environment
   - Housing, location, international support, advising, safety, transportation, and student life.
9. Outcomes and pathway evidence
   - Career, graduation, college placement, graduate-school, alumni, or official outcome evidence.
10. Risk register
   - Risk, severity, evidence, and mitigation.
11. Source log
   - Source title, URL, accessed date, and supported claim.

## Visual Standards

- Use a clear report hierarchy: large cover title, section headings, compact tables, and callout boxes.
- Make the first two pages decision-oriented; put detailed evidence later.
- Prefer tables for requirements, costs, risks, and source logs.
- Use a small, restrained color palette with high contrast.
- Use page numbers and a report date.
- Keep direct quotes short. Put source URLs in the source log rather than in dense body text.

## Required PDF Checks

Before delivery:

- Confirm the PDF opens.
- Inspect at least the cover, a middle page, and the final source-log page.
- Check that Chinese text renders if the report is in Chinese.
- Check tables for clipped text or overlapping cells.
- Confirm every unstable fact has a source or an uncertainty note.

If local PDF rendering tools are unavailable, still generate the PDF and tell the user which visual checks could not be completed.
