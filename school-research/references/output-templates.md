# Single-School Research Output Templates

## Quick School Profile

Use this structure for a concise answer:

- Bottom line: 2-3 sentences on whether the school looks worth deeper consideration.
- Best-fit reasons: 3-5 bullets.
- Main concerns: 3-5 bullets.
- Key admissions facts: deadline, route, requirements, testing, English policy, and special materials.
- Cost and aid snapshot.
- Evidence gaps.
- Next actions.

## Deep School Profile

Use this structure for one school:

1. Bottom line
   - 2-3 sentences on whether this school is worth keeping and why.
   - Confidence level: high, medium, or low, based on source coverage.

2. School identity
   - Official name, campus, location, school type, student level, and relevant college or department.

3. Program fit
   - Major or curriculum match.
   - Distinctive academic opportunities.
   - Gaps or risks.

4. Admissions facts
   - Application route.
   - Requirements.
   - Deadlines.
   - Testing and English policy.
   - International applicant notes.

5. Cost and aid
   - Tuition and living-cost estimate.
   - Aid or scholarship eligibility.
   - Financial risk notes.

6. Student environment
   - Housing, location, international support, student life, advising, safety, and transportation.

7. Outcomes and pathway evidence
   - Career, graduation, college placement, graduate school, alumni, or official outcomes data.

8. Fit judgment
   - Academic fit.
   - Admissions competitiveness.
   - Financial fit.
   - Environment fit.
   - Portfolio role.

9. Risk register
   - Severity, evidence, and mitigation for each risk.

10. Source log
   - Source title, URL, accessed date, and what the source supports.

11. Next actions
   - Specific tasks such as email admissions, verify deadline cycle, request transcript mapping, draft essays, or schedule visit/interview.

## Application Checklist

Use this structure when the user wants to apply:

- Application platform and account setup
- Deadline and decision plan
- Transcript or school report requirements
- Testing and English proficiency requirements
- Essays, supplements, writing samples, portfolio, audition, or interview
- Recommendation letters
- Financial aid or scholarship forms
- Visa or international-student tasks
- Questions to email the school
