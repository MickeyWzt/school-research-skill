# FAQ

## What does this skill produce?

It helps create source-backed school research, admissions due diligence, and polished PDF-style reports for one school, college, university, boarding school, international school, or academic program.

## Why does it focus on one school at a time?

Deep school research depends on exact campus, program, applicant category, entry term, tuition rules, deadlines, and source quality. One-school scope keeps the report specific enough to be useful.

## Does it replace official admissions advice?

No. It is a research and planning aid. Always verify final requirements, dates, fees, scholarships, and visa or residency rules with official school and government sources.

## Why are sources so important?

Admissions and cost information changes often. The skill prioritizes official pages and asks the report to show what was checked, what could not be found, and where uncertainty remains.

## Can it compare multiple schools?

The best workflow is to produce one strong report per school, then compare the outputs. That keeps each profile grounded in sources instead of becoming a shallow list.

## What should I include in a bug report?

Include the school name, campus or program, applicant category, entry year, the command or prompt used, the expected report section, and any source URLs that were missed or handled incorrectly.
